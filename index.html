WELCOME TO HARBOR CITY

How to play

There is no wrong way to spend a day.

01Move

WASD to walk, Shift to run. On mobile, use the left joystick.

02Look

Move the mouse or drag the right side of the screen. Click the world to lock the cursor.

03Interact

E enters shops, talks to people, and gets into cars. The prompt at the bottom shows what is nearby.

04Live

Eat when hungry, sleep when tired, shop for clothes, adopt a pet, buy a car, and buy Maple House.

05Work

Open the Jobs tab or visit CityWorks. Courier, cab, and café shifts all pay cash.

06Phone

Tab opens your phone: map, bag, wardrobe, home, jobs, and settings.

Take your time. The city keeps moving.
